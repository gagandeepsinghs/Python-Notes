Core Concepts & Principles

  • 99% Accuracy with no fraud detected: Class imbalance problem; inspect confusion matrix, Recall, PR-AUC, and class weights.
  • Train 99%, Validation 72%: Overfitting / Data Leakage; add regularization, simplify model, or increase training data.
  • 90-Day Study Roadmap: Days 1-15 (Python/NumPy) -> Days 16-30 (Pandas/EDA/Stats) -> Days 31-45 (ML) -> Days 46-60 (Advanced ML) -> Days 61-75 (DL/AI) -> Days 76-90 (Projects/Deployment).
