Chapter 1

What are Conditional Statements

Conditional Statements woh hoti hai jo program ko decision lene me help karti hai. Executions dynamic control flow provide karti hain depend karta hai ki specific condition satisfied (True) hai ya unsatisfied (False).

Core Concept

Control flow decision-making relies on comparison operators (==, !=, >, <, >=, <=) and logical operators (and, or, not).
