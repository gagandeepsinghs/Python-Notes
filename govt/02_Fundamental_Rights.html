2 Short Tricks & Mnemonics
💡 Equality Trick: E-D-O-U-T (Art 14-18)
E-Equality (14), D-Discrimination (15), O-Opportunity (16), U-Untouchability (17), T-Titles (18).

💡 Art 19 6 Freedoms Trick: S-A-A-M-R-P
19(1)(a) Speech ➔ (b) Assembly ➔ (c) Association ➔ (d) Movement ➔ (e) Residence ➔ (g) Profession.
3 Fundamental Rights PYQ MCQs
Q1 (SSC CGL 2023): Right to Education (Art 21A) was added by which amendment?

A) 42nd Amendment | B) 44th Amendment | C) 86th Amendment | D) 91st Amendment

Answer: C (86th Amendment 2002) - Provides free & compulsory education for age 6-14.
