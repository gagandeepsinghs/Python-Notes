⚡ The Magic +89 & +90 Formula (Union ➔ State Articles)
💡 Hinglish Explanation: Tumhe State ke saare alag Articles yaad karne ki bilkul zarurat nahi hai! Bas Centre/Union ke Articles mein +89 (ya +90) add kar do, toh exact State wala Article apne aap nikal aayega.

Art 72: President Pardoning Power (President ki Maafi) 72 + 89 = 161 Art 161: Governor Pardoning Power (Governor ki Maafi)
Art 74: Council of Ministers (PM ki Mantri Parishad) 74 + 89 = 163 Art 163: Council of Ministers (CM ki Mantri Parishad)
Art 75: PM & Ministers Appointment 75 + 89 = 164 Art 164: CM & State Ministers Appointment
Art 76: Attorney General of India (Desh ka sabse bada Vakil) 76 + 89 = 165 Art 165: Advocate General for State (State ka sabse bada Vakil)
Art 110: Money Bill in Parliament 110 + 89 = 199 Art 199: Money Bill in State Vidhan Sabha
Art 112: Union Budget (Annual Financial Statement) 112 + 90 = 202 Art 202: State Budget

PART I & II • ART 1 TO 11
1 Union & Its Territory (1-4) & Citizenship (5-11)
💡 Hinglish Notes:
• Article 1: Direct bolta hai - "India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States" (Bharat States ka ek Sangh hai).
• Article 9: Agar koi Indian citizen kisi dusre country ki citizenship leta hai, toh uski Indian citizenship automatically khatam ho jati hai (Single Citizenship policy borrowed from UK).

Art 1 Union Name

  • India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States.

Art 2 New States

  • Naye territory ko India mein add karna (outside India).

Art 3 Boundaries

  • Existing States ki boundary, name ya area change karna (e.g., Telangana creation).

Art 9 & 11 Citizenship

  • Art 9: Single Citizenship (Dusre desh ki citizenship par Indian citizenship cancel).
  • Art 11: Parliament citizenship rules bana sakti hai (Citizenship Act 1955).
PART III • ART 12 TO 35
2 Fundamental Rights (Maulik Adhikar) Super High Frequency
💡 Trick 1 - Right to Equality (Art 14-18): "E-D-O-U-T"
• Equality before Law (Art 14)
• Discrimination Prohibition (Art 15 - Dharam, Jaati, Sex ke aadhar par koi bhedbhav nahi)
• Opportunity in Jobs (Art 16)
• Untouchability Abolition (Art 17 - Chhuachhoot ka Ant: SSC ka favorite question!)
• Titles Abolition (Art 18 - Sir/Rai Bahadur jaise titles par ban)
💡 Hinglish Notes - Art 19 6 Freedoms Trick (S-A-A-M-R-P):
19(1)(a) **S**peech (Bolne ki azadi) ➔ 19(1)(b) **A**ssembly (Gather hone ki azadi) ➔ 19(1)(c) **A**ssociations (Union banane ki azadi) ➔ 19(1)(d) **M**ovement (Desh mein ghoomne ki azadi) ➔ 19(1)(e) **R**esidence (Rehne ki azadi) ➔ 19(1)(g) **P**rofession (Kamaane / Job karne ki azadi).
Article Subject Matter Hinglish Key Concept & SSC Facts
Article 20 Protection in conviction Ek gunah ki ek hi baar saza (No Double Jeopardy). Apne khilaf gawahi ke liye force nahi kar sakte.
Article 21 Right to Life & Liberty Jine ka Adhikar. Right to Privacy (K.S. Puttaswamy case) aur Swachh Hawa/Pani ka adhikar isi mein aata hai.
Article 21A Right to Education (RTE) 86th Amendment 2002 se add hua. 6 se 14 saal ke bacho ko Muft aur Anivarya Shiksha.
Article 23 Human Trafficking & Begar Insaano ki khareed-farokht aur majboori mein bina paise ke kaam karwana (Begar) prohibited hai.
Article 24 Child Labor Prohibition 14 saal se kam umar ke bacho ko factory ya dangerous mines mein kaam karwana crime hai.
Article 32 Constitutional Remedies Dr. B.R. Ambedkar ne ise "Samvidhan ki Aatma aur Hriday" (Heart & Soul) kaha tha. SC 5 Writs issue karta hai.
PART IV & IV-A • ART 36 TO 51A
3 DPSP & Fundamental Duties (Kartavya)
💡 Short Tricks for DPSP Articles (Hinglish):
• Art 40 = Gram Panchayat (4+0 = Gaon ki Panchayat ke 4 kone)
• Art 44 = Uniform Civil Code (UCC) (4 aur 4 ek se Uniform dikhte hain)
• Art 48A = Environment Protection ('A' for Air & Atmosphere - 42nd Amendment 1976)
• Art 50 = Judiciary & Executive Separation (50-50 Split: Judge aur Mantri ka kaam alag)
• Art 51 = International Peace (Duniya mein shanti ke liye ₹51 ka Shagun!)
🇮🇳 Article 51A - Fundamental Duties (Maulik Kartavya):
• Swaran Singh Committee ki recommendation par 42nd Amendment 1976 se add hue.
• Phele 10 duties the; 11th duty (6-14 age child education) 86th Amendment 2002 se add hui. Borrowed from USSR.
PART V • ART 52 TO 123
4 President of India Articles (Trick: P-E-E-M-T-E-Q-O-I)
💡 President Important Articles (Hinglish):
• Art 52: Desh mein ek President honge (First Citizen of India).
• Art 54: Election (Chunaav) - Isme Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha aur State Assemblies ke ONLY ELECTED members vote dete hain.
• Art 61: Impeachment (Hataane ka tarika) - Jab Samvidhan ka violation ho. 14 din ka advance notice Dena zaruri hai.
• Art 72: Pardoning Power (Maafi ka Adhikar) - Phansi (Death Penalty) aur Court Martial ko maaf kar sakte hain.
• Art 123: Ordinance (Adhyadesh) - Jab Parliament session mein na ho tab kanoon banana.
Article Subject Matter SSC & Hinglish Key Facts
Article 52 President of India India ke Pratham Nagrik (First Citizen). Supreme Commander of Armed Forces.
Article 54 President Election Electoral College: LS, RS aur State Assemblies ke Elected members.
Article 61 Impeachment President ko hatane ki process (Violation of Constitution par).
Article 72 Pardoning Power Phansi ki saza sirf President maaf kar sakte hain (Governor nahi).
Article 123 Ordinance Power Ordinance ki max duration 6 weeks (from reassembly of Parliament) hoti hai.
PART V • ART 79 TO 122
5 Parliament Articles (SARAL / सरल Trick)
💡 Trick - S-A-R-A-L (सरल):
• स (S) = Samsad / Parliament ➔ Article 79
• र (R) = Rajya Sabha ➔ Article 80
• ल (L) = Lok Sabha ➔ Article 81

Art 108 Joint Sitting

  • President bulate hain jab kisi Bill par deadlock ho.
  • Preside humesha Lok Sabha Speaker karte hain!

Art 110 Money Bill

  • Decide sirf Lok Sabha Speaker karte hain ki bill Money Bill hai ya nahi.
  • Rajya Sabha ise max 14 din tak rok sakti hai.

Art 112 Budget

  • Official naam: Annual Financial Statement.
  • Constitution mein 'Budget' word kahin nahi likha hai!
