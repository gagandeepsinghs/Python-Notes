HISTORY • ANCIENT
1 Indus Valley Civilization (IVC Sites & Special Findings)
💡 Hinglish Summary: Harappan Civilization apni Grid Town Planning aur Subterranean Drainage ke liye famous thi.
• Harappa: Daya Ram Sahni ne 1921 mein Ravi river ke kinare khoja tha (6 Granaries mili theen).
• Mohenjo-daro: R.D. Banerjee ne 1922 mein Indus river ke kinare khoja (Great Bath aur Bronze Dancing Girl mili).
• Lothal (Gujarat): Duniya ka sabse purana Tidal Dockyard tha.
• Rakhigarhi (Haryana): India mein sabse bada Indus Valley site hai.
• Important Fact: Harappan logon ko Bronze, Copper, Gold ka pata tha — lekin IRON (Loha) bilkul UNKNOWN tha!

POLITY • AMENDMENTS
6 Most Asked Constitutional Amendments (Hinglish Summary)
💡 Polity Amendments Tricks:
• 42nd Amendment (1976): Ise "Mini Constitution" kehte hain. Preamble mein 3 naye words (Socialist, Secular, Integrity) aur Fundamental Duties (Art 51A)isi se add hue the.
• 44th Amendment (1978): Right to Property ko Fundamental Right se hata kar Art 300A ke under ek Legal Right bana diya.
• 61st Amendment (1989): Voting age 21 saal se ghata kar 18 saal kar di gayi.
• 73rd & 74th Amendments (1992): Panchayati Raj aur Municipalities ko constitutional status diya gaya.
• 86th Amendment (2002): 6-14 saal ke bacho ke liye Right to Education (Art 21A) add hua.
• 101st Amendment (2016): GST (Goods & Services Tax) Art 279A ke under laya gaya.
GEOGRAPHY • RIVERS
11 Panch Prayag River Confluences (Hinglish Trick)
💡 Panch Prayag Hinglish Shortcut: River Alaknanda main stream hai jo badhti rehti hai:
1. Alaknanda + Dhauliganga = Vishnuprayag
2. Alaknanda + Nandakini = Nandaprayag
3. Alaknanda + Pindar = Karnaprayag
4. Alaknanda + Mandakini = Rudraprayag
5. Alaknanda + Bhagirathi = Devprayag ➔ Yahan se aage ise GANGA kehte hain!

Q1. Which constitutional amendment act lowered the voting age in India from 21 years to 18 years?
A) 44th Amendment Act
B) 61st Amendment Act
C) 73rd Amendment Act
D) 86th Amendment Act
Correct Answer: Option B (61st Amendment Act, 1989)
Hinglish Explanation: Rajiv Gandhi government ke time 61st Constitutional Amendment Act (1989) laya gaya tha jisse Article 326 mein badlav karke voting age ko 21 saal se ghata kar 18 saal kar diya gaya.
SSC CGL 2023
Q2. Which river is the only river in India that cuts the Tropic of Cancer (Kark Rekha) twice?
A) Narmada River
B) Mahi River
C) Tapti River
D) Luni River
Correct Answer: Option B (Mahi River)
Hinglish Explanation: Mahi River Madhya Pradesh ke Vindhya range se nikalti hai, Rajasthan se hote hue Gujarat mein Gulf of Khambhat mein girti hai. Ye India ki akeli nadi hai jo Tropic of Cancer ko 2 baar cut karti hai.
