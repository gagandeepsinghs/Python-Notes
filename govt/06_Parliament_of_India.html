1 SARAL Trick (Art 79, 80, 81)
💡 SARAL Trick (सरल):
• S = Samsad / Parliament ➔ Article 79 (President + LS + RS)
• R = Rajya Sabha ➔ Article 80 (Upper House, Max 250)
• L = Lok Sabha ➔ Article 81 (Lower House, Max 550)

3 Parliament PYQ MCQs
Q1 (SSC CGL 2023): Who presides over a Joint Sitting of Parliament under Article 108?

A) President | B) Vice President | C) Speaker of Lok Sabha | D) Prime Minister

Answer: C (Speaker of Lok Sabha) - Joint sitting is presided over by the Speaker of Lok Sabha.
