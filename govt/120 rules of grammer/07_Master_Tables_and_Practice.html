SSC Error-Spotting Mini Practice

Hinglish Explanation: Original SSC-style practice for application of the rules.

1. Each of the boys have a notebook.

Error: has.
Rule: 'Each' takes a singular verb.

2. He did not went to school.

Error: go.
Rule: Did + V1.

3. She is senior than me.

Error: to.
Rule: Senior + to.

4. I look forward to meet you.

Error: meeting.
Rule: 'To' is a preposition here, so use V-ing.

5. One of my friends are a doctor.

Error: is.
Rule: 'One of' takes a plural noun but a singular verb.

6. He is good in English.

Error: at.
Rule: Good at (skill).

7. Neither Ram nor his friends is ready.

Error: are.
Rule: Verb agrees with the nearest subject ("friends").

8. She has been study for two hours.

Error: studying.
Rule: Present perfect continuous takes V-ing.

9. He is a honest man.

Error: an honest.
Rule: Honest starts with a vowel sound.

10. Let's go, will we?

Error: shall we?
Rule: 'Let's' takes the tag 'shall we?'.
GOLDEN SSC RULE FOR ERROR SPOTTING:
When stuck, use this fixed scan order to save time:
Subject → Verb → Tense → Pronoun → Article → Preposition → Comparison → Conjunction → Parallelism.
