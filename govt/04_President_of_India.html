Articles 52 to 62 • Executive Head • Pardoning Power & Impeachment • Bilingual

1 Core Articles & Qualifications
💡 Hinglish Notes:
• Art 52: India ka ek President hoga (First Citizen of India).
• Art 54 (Election): Electoral College mein Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha aur State Vidhan Sabha ke ONLY ELECTED members vote dete hain (Nominated members vote NAHI dete).
• Qualification: Min 35 saal age, Indian Citizen, Lok Sabha member banne ki qualification honi chahiye.
• Art 60: Oath (Shapath) CJI (Chief Justice of India) dilate hain.

3 President PYQ MCQs
Q1 (SSC CGL 2022): Under which Article is the President empowered to grant pardons and commute death sentences?

A) Article 61 | B) Article 72 | C) Article 123 | D) Article 143

Answer: B (Article 72) - President has pardoning power under Art 72.
